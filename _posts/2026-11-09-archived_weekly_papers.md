#### **Title:
          Quasi-Normal Modes of Covariant Loop Quantum Black Holes**
 - **Authors:** Chao Zhang, Sushant G. Ghosh, Anzhong Wang
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:
General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc); High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th)
 - **Arxiv link:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/](https://arxiv.org/abs/)
 - **Abstract**
 We investigate the quasinormal modes (QNMs) and time-domain response of a massless, minimally coupled test scalar field on static, spherically symmetric covariant loop quantum black hole (cLQBH) backgrounds. Focusing on a phenomenological, Gaussian-localized holonomy profile, we obtain a localized deformation of the scalar effective potential containing both positive and negative parts. This provides a geometric realization of the bump effect, with the potential deformation and tortoise-coordinate mapping determined consistently by the same background. We characterize the deformation by its location, amplitude, and width, and study its effects using frequency-domain shooting and time-domain evolution, supplemented by matrix-pencil mode extraction and waveform reconstruction. The QNM spectrum exhibits spiral trajectories as the bump location varies, enhanced sensitivity of the overtones, and mode overtaking, whereby the branch continued from the first Schwarzschild overtone becomes longer-lived than the continued fundamental branch. Despite substantial spectral changes, the early response can remain close to its Schwarzschild counterpart, with more pronounced differences emerging later. To quantify this behavior, we introduce a local waveform-deviation time and compare it with a relative-amplitude faintness time, thereby defining an operational observation window. The deviation time depends nonmonotonically on the bump location, and suitable parameter choices allow appreciable departures to emerge before the adopted faintness threshold is reached. These results connect localized holonomy corrections with spectral sensitivity and waveform evolution, providing a basis for future gravitational-perturbation studies and assessments of gravitational-wave detectability. 

#### Spin foam related papers

#### **Title:
          Inner Product of Closed Universe from Gravitational Path-integral**
 - **Authors:** Masamichi Miyaji, Sunil Sake, Kotaro Shinmyo
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:
High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th)
 - **Arxiv link:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/](https://arxiv.org/abs/)
 - **Abstract**
 We construct the Hilbert space of a closed universe of fixed topology in anti-de Sitter (AdS) and de Sitter (dS) Jackiw-Teitelboim (JT) gravity from the gravitational path integral. Quantizing gravity involves several subtleties, including the choice of lapse contour, path-integral measure, operator ordering, global structure of the gauge group, the implementation of gravitational constraints and the gauge symmetries at initial and final timeslices. We develop a formalism that addresses these issues and determines the inner product of physical states, providing a framework to assign probabilities to cosmological wavefunctions. For a fixed topology and a lapse contour, we compute the inner product exactly and obtain the corresponding resolution of the identity in JT gravity. We demonstrate that the co-invariant and invariant Hilbert spaces in refined algebraic quantization arise from particular gauge fixing at the initial and final timeslices. In particular, the formalism provides precise meaning for a gravitational wavefunction with fixed geometric data as a co-invariant state, including its integral measure. These constructions can be formulated within the Hamiltonian BFV-BRST formalism. We also discuss the global structure of the Hilbert space, which differs from that of BF theory and depends on the prescription adopted near the big bang/crunch. Our results imply that the cylinder geometry captures the gravitational inner product for all perturbative states, including the Hartle-Hawking no-boundary state.

#### **Title:
          Primordial Gravitational Waves in Loop Quantum Cosmology: Regularization Ambiguities and Initial State Dependence**
 - **Authors:** Mah Noor Jamil, Parampreet Singh
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:
General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc); Cosmology and Nongalactic Astrophysics (astro-ph.CO)
 - **Arxiv link:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/](https://arxiv.org/abs/)
 - **Abstract**
 We compute the primordial tensor power spectrum in standard loop quantum cosmology and in the modified models mLQC-I and mLQC-II, evolving the tensor perturbations across the bounce in the hybrid and dressed metric prescriptions with a Starobinsky potential. In mLQC-I, the emergent de Sitter contracting branch requires the use of dedicated initial states. We use de Sitter mode function in the dressed metric approach and a Hankel-function solution in the hybrid approach. For inflationary e-foldings $N_e = 60$ and $65$, the pivot-scale tensor-to-scalar ratio agrees with the value of standard inflation with this potential. The scale-dependent ratio $r(k)=\mathcal{P}_T/\mathcal{P}_S$ cancels effects common to the two sectors and separates features that are independent of the initial state from those that depend on it. It varies slowly in the scale-invariant regime and oscillates around $r_\star$ at smaller $k$. The oscillations next to the scale-invariant regime do not depend on the adiabatic order, unlike the peaks and spikes at lower $k$. For a given initial state, the regularization dependence survives in the infrared, where long-wavelength modes retain a record of the bounce and of the pre-bounce branch. We propagate the normalized spectra to the CMB $B$-mode spectrum and discuss observational prospects.
