#### **Title:
          Non-singular cosmology from effective LQG-inspired black holes**
 - **Authors:** V. F. C. Belo, F. G. S. Menezes, S. M. Rupf, I. P. R. Baranov, H. A. Borges
 - **Subjects:** Subjects:
General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc); High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th)
 - **Arxiv link:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/](https://arxiv.org/abs/)
 - **Abstract**
 We propose a non-singular primordial cosmology derived from matching an effective Loop Quantum Gravity-inspired black hole geometry to an expanding Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker universe via Darmois-Israel junction conditions. The resulting modified Friedmann equation replaces the singularity with a quantum bounce in the Planckian regime. Mapping the surface energy-momentum tensor of the dynamic thin shell onto a homogeneous scalar field reveals a transient phantom-like behavior. For any equation of state of the shell satisfying $\omega > -1$, its effective energy density is diluted with cosmic expansion. Extending the framework to a two-scalar-field system provides a mechanism to bridge the energy gap between the Planckian bounce and the sub-Planckian scale. The pre-inflationary phase is brief, enabling the second scalar field to take over to drive the standard slow-roll inflation.
